# Publication boundaries

Never edit source notes to make them fit a story. Never treat `authoring/` as
published. Never remove `draft: true`, move a file into a published collection,
or claim a page is live without explicit human authorization and validation.

A reviewer may approve readiness; only the separately authorized publication
step crosses the boundary.

Speaker notes bundled in the site are public even if shown in a presenter view.
Never put sensitive information or raw source notes there. Curated factual
notes and reveals require evidence, human outline approval, and publication
review. Presenter-specific details are stored locally; do not represent local
storage as a guarantee of secrecy.
